A day with Uhifadhi Doria, and what leaves your phone

The statement in the order your day happens. Each step says what the app records at that moment and where it goes. Everything lands on your organisation’s own installation of uhifadhi; Utafiti Labs makes the app and never receives the records.

  1. 06:40You sign in

    Your identifier and a token for this phone go to the installation. The roster tells the app your name, rank and posting; the app adds nothing to them.

  2. 06:52You check in

    Your status and the phone's position at that moment are sent. From now until you check out, a position ping goes every few minutes, at the interval your area set, with the system's location indicator on the whole time.

  3. 08:10You start a patrol

    Position fixes are recorded to the phone as you walk. Nothing leaves the phone until it has signal and you sync.

  4. 09:35You photograph a snare

    The camera opens only when you press the shutter. The picture is stamped with the place and time and attached to the observation, on the phone.

  5. 13:20You sync at the post

    Patrols, tracks, observations and photographs go to your organisation's installation over HTTPS. The phone keeps every record until the installation confirms it arrived.

  6. 17:05You check out

    Your check-out and handover note are sent, and the pings stop. Signing out was refused until now, so the day is never left half-reported.

  7. Any timeThe app crashes

    A report goes to the installation's monitoring: the phone's model and system, the screen and the action, your identifier, and the error. Passwords, tokens and photo bytes are stripped before it leaves.

Who this is about

The people who carry Uhifadhi Doria on patrol and on duty, and the organisation whose records they keep.

Your organisation runs its own installation of uhifadhi and is responsible for every record the app sends to it. The app is made by Utafiti Labs, which writes the software and never receives the records: nothing the phone collects goes anywhere but your organisation's installation.

You use the app because your organisation gave you an account. Questions about your records, and requests to see or correct them, go to the administrator of your organisation, not to the maker.

What stays on the phone

The phone is the original until the installation has acknowledged a record.

On the trail there is no network, so the phone keeps every record until the installation confirms it arrived. Nothing is deleted from the phone before then.

Signing out is refused while you are checked in, so a day on duty is never left half-reported. Uninstalling the app deletes any record that has not yet been sent. Sync first.

What the app does not do

Said plainly, because a reader should not have to infer it.

No advertising, no analytics
There is no advertising, no analytics service and no measurement of how you use the app. The only report the app makes about itself is a crash report.
No third parties
Nothing is sold, shared or passed to anyone outside your organisation's installation, apart from the map tiles described above.
No tracking across apps
The app reads nothing from other apps and leaves nothing for them to read.
No location off duty
Background location is used only while a patrol is being recorded or while you are checked in, and the system shows its indicator the whole time.

What the phone will ask you

Each permission is asked once, at the moment it is first needed, with the reason on the screen.

Location
To record where your patrol went and to stamp an observation with the spot you saw it. While using the app, with background use during a patrol or a duty watch.
Camera
To photograph what you find. Only when you press the shutter.
Photos
Only on a device with no camera, to pick one existing picture. The library is read, never written.
Notifications
To remind you of a check-in or a check-out you have not made.

How long it is kept

For as long as your organisation's own rules for its records say.

The records belong to your organisation and are kept under its records policy, the same as a patrol written on paper. To see what is held about you, to correct it, or to ask for it to be removed, speak to your organisation's administrator, who can act on the installation directly.

Changes to this statement

When the app starts collecting something new, or stops, the statement changes with that release and the date at the top changes with it. An installation always shows the statement for the version it accepts.
